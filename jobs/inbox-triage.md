# Inbox triage (requires a connected email provider)

Review new inbox messages from the past business day. Group them into urgent,
waiting for reply, and informational. Identify actionable deadlines and draft
brief replies for my review. Never send, delete, or archive messages automatically.
Avoid quoting confidential message contents in public logs or repositories.
If email access isn't available, report the missing connection instead of guessing.
